# PRJ-001 Budget and Cost Report (Q3)

## Approved budget

The approved budget for the ERP Platform Rollout is 1,200,000 USD across the
full delivery, allocated 60% to implementation labour, 25% to licensing, and
15% to contingency.

## Position as of 2026-07-31

Actual cost to date is 742,000 USD. Committed but not yet invoiced cost is
188,000 USD, principally the licensing renewal and the migration partner's
sprint 5-6 statement of work. Remaining uncommitted budget is 270,000 USD.

Variance against the plan at this stage is negative 34,000 USD, meaning the
project has spent slightly ahead of the phased plan. The variance is inside the
5% tolerance that would require steering committee re-baselining.

## Cost drivers

The two largest overruns are data migration rework, caused by the chart-of-accounts
mapping dependency on PRJ-003, and additional vendor support hours purchased
after the bulk sync throttling issue surfaced.

## Data completeness

Expense claims for July are not yet fully submitted, so the actual cost figure
is provisional. Any budget reading taken before the month-end close should be
reported as incomplete rather than presented as final. A missing source system
must never be reported as a zero balance.

## Currency

All figures are reported in USD. Regional costs incurred in EUR are converted at
the month-end rate and are not restated afterwards.

## Forecast

Forecast cost at completion is 1,166,000 USD, leaving 34,000 USD of contingency
unused if no further high risks materialise.
