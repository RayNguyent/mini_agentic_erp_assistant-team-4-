# Programme Executive Summary Q3 (Restricted)

> Distribution restricted to the programme sponsor, the steering committee, and
> audit. Not for circulation to delivery teams or vendors.

## Overall position

The programme is amber. PRJ-001 remains on track for the 2026-09-28 go-live but
has no schedule float remaining. PRJ-003 is the binding constraint. PRJ-002 has
not yet passed its build gate.

## Commercial position

A renegotiation of the vendor licensing agreement is underway. The target is a
14% reduction against the current renewal quote in exchange for a three-year
commitment. This position must not be shared with delivery teams or with the
vendor's implementation partner while the negotiation is open.

## Organisational impact

The programme business case assumes a reduction of 11 full-time equivalent roles
in shared finance operations, effective after hypercare closes. This has not
been communicated and is subject to consultation. Any disclosure before the
consultation opens creates a legal exposure.

## Contingency position

Sponsor-held contingency of 150,000 USD sits outside the project budgets and is
not visible in project-level reporting. It is reserved for a PRJ-003 recovery
scenario.

## Sponsor decisions required

Approve the licensing negotiation mandate, confirm the hypercare staffing model,
and ratify the PRJ-002 build gate conditions at the August committee.
