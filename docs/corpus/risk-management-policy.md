# Project Risk Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how delivery risks are recorded, rated, owned, and escalated
across all projects in the programme. It applies to PRJ-001, PRJ-002, and
PRJ-003 equally.

## Severity ratings

Risks are rated **low**, **medium**, or **high**. There is no critical tier;
anything that would qualify as critical is a high risk with an immediate
escalation flag.

- **Low** — impact is absorbed within the current sprint, no date or budget
  effect. Reviewed monthly.
- **Medium** — impact requires re-planning within the current milestone, or a
  budget effect under 5%. Reviewed fortnightly at the steering committee.
- **High** — impact threatens the go-live date, exceeds 5% of approved budget,
  or introduces a regulatory or data-protection exposure. Reviewed weekly and
  escalated to the sponsor within two business days of being raised.

## Ownership

Every risk has a named owner who is accountable for the mitigation, not merely
informed of it. A risk without an owner is invalid and must be rejected at
intake. The project manager owns the register; they do not own every risk.

## Risk states

A risk moves through **Open**, **Mitigated**, and **Closed**. A risk may only be
closed by its owner or the project manager, and closing requires a recorded
rationale. Mitigated means the mitigation is in place but the underlying
condition still exists.

## Intake requirements

Raising a risk requires a title, a description of the condition and its
consequence, a probability, an impact, a severity, and an owner. Risks raised
without a description are returned to the raiser rather than recorded.

## Segregation of duties

The person raising a risk may not be the sole approver of a change that closes
it. Any automated or assistant-driven creation of a risk record requires
explicit human approval before it is written, and the approving human must be
recorded alongside the record.

## Review cadence

The full register is reviewed at every sprint review. High risks are reviewed
weekly regardless of sprint boundaries.
